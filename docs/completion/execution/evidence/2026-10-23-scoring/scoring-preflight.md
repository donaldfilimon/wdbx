## Required frozen behavior

Sources: `docs/completion/designs.json:38-49` (scoring, D06-D08), `:76-85` (ATP, D10), `:138-149` (scheduler, D15/D16/D18); architecture authority is `specimen-studio/public/WDBX-Specimen-Architecture-Specification.md` (hereafter Architecture).

- Scanner evidence is already a separate pure mechanism. Preserve exact checked i128 rational S/D/difference and one final clamp; weights are 40/20/20/20 and absent features supply 0S/fullD. Do not round or independently clamp S/D. Scanner confidence is evidence, not probability (`scanner_v3/mod.rs:27-44,156-167,210-235`; `scanner_v3/rational.rs:84-100`; local profile `docs/completion/execution/language-v3-profile.md:112-139`). New downstream policy must not alter these selected semantics.
- Confidence order: `Cbase=clamp(Cscan-10*a*max(0,-v),-100,100)`; determine baseline qualification `Cbase>=threshold`; decide jitter eligibility from this baseline; then `Ceff=clamp(Cbase+signed_delta,-100,100)`; final inclusive gate `Ceff>=threshold` plus local policy gates. Strength must be finite, admissible only at `>0`, and influences arbitration weight rather than bounded confidence. Defaults: threshold 50, maximum strength 100 (`designs.json:46`; Architecture `:581-607`).
- Jitter: independent fair sign and uniform magnitude `[0,1]`, brainstorm `[0,5]`; disabled toggle, exact baseline ±100, and maximum-strength baseline-qualified entries consume no jitter. Maximum-strength subthreshold entries remain eligible. Draws are temporary, traceable, and never change baseline/durable state. Endpoint/crystallization tests concern the ATP-modulated baseline, not an early scanner endpoint (`designs.json:46`; Architecture `:585-605`).
- Entry alternative election precedes contribution submission: filter inhibited/ineligible alternatives, validate finite nonnegative weights, choose proportionally; zero-total policy must be explicit. The scoring bundle selects no action for zero redundant-election total. Preserve every independently qualified entry: there is no one-vote-per-node or global winner (`Architecture:609-637`; `designs.json:46`).
- Redundancy is exact same origin chunk + fanout lineage + canonical action AST + inhibition set + captures. Preserve complementary actions, distinct intermediate facts and attached groups. For a real redundant group use finite f64 `max(0,Ceff)*strength`, reject nonfinite weight/total, select none at zero total, and trace every candidate, draw and winner. Do not group merely by node ID, origin or normalized output text (`designs.json:46`; Architecture `:625-637`).
- Attachment: hard means always schedule the target on the actual unmatched remainder, not force a vote. Soft uses configured affinity sampling. Target independently gates, receives preserved inhibition/constraints/captures, and contributes to an exempt relationship group. Track visits and bounded depth/progress and enqueue rather than recurse. Qualifying issuer contribution is the architecture's recommended trigger, not a frozen selected numeric profile (`Architecture:647-679`). Existing native profile has four hops (`:683`).
- Lifecycle: intake immutable context/ATP, preparation/retrieval, all admitted reasoning and attachment work, finish voter phase or explicitly cancel/report partial, scoped redundancy arbitration, support retrieval/context filtering, compose steps/constraints, resolve scripts using original captures, optional transient synthesis, bounded tone variation, output provenance, aftercare and teardown (`Architecture:689-735`). No effects should escape from a cancelled queue (`designs.json:48`).
- Shared work: one specimen-wide 1,000 active-comparison cap across foreground, attachments and maintenance. Admit all-or-backpressure within max 100,000 pending comparisons; execute every admitted comparison. Weighted round robin 8 foreground quanta:1 maintenance, FIFO within queue, chunks ≤4,096 with checkpoints/cancellation; release permits on cancellation. A local sequential mechanism may model permits/fairness, but cannot establish real host concurrency or cancellation effects (`designs.json:47-48,145-149`; Architecture `:1228-1234,1353-1357`).

## ATP and input policy

- Validate finite `v∈[-1,1]`, `a∈[0,1]`, cooldown and stimulus state; reject backwards elapsed. Clamp elapsed to `[0,3600]`; `d=exp(-dt/180)`, `v0=d*v`, `a0=d*a`. Use libm in the kernel. When cooldown is positive, decay only and subtract dt (bounded at zero); otherwise `v1=clamp(v0+.2*stimulus.valence*stimulus.intensity,-1,1)`, `a1=clamp(a0+.2*intensity+.1*urgency,0,1)`. At `a1>=.85`, publish `a=.5*a1`, `v=.8*v1`, cooldown=30 seconds; otherwise retain v1/a1 (`designs.json:81-84`).
- Urgency is an explicit user urgent flag or Chargebook match with valence ≤-.7 and intensity ≥.8 in an active clause. Quoted and negated stimuli are excluded. Unknown charge is zero. Urgency does not grant authority or widen budgets. Autonomous input is disabled by default; enabling requires internal-stimulus provenance with the same capability/inhibition checks (`designs.json:82-84`; Architecture `:882-892`).
- Ephemeral tonal jitter is independent of confidence jitter: ±uniform `[0,.01]`, brainstorm max `.05`; never integrate it. Tone replacement only for table-equivalent AST meanings with charge distance ≤.5; at most three ranked synonym attempts then original. Numeric, negated, quoted/code and procedural spans are immutable. Reward is clamped `[-.1,.1]` before integration, but its mapping into state is not supplied (`designs.json:82-84`).
- Current prepared actions expose immutable original/spans/origin/captures/operators, charges and policy guards. `PreparedAction::executable` requires uninhibited, resolved frame and no/refuted-free conditional disposition (`language_v3/model.rs:303-338`, specifically `:315-321`). `CandidateOrigin.constraints` retains the corresponding guard facts; a declared bypass never authorizes an inhibited action (`scanner_v3/routes/model.rs:118-155`; language profile `:141-150`). Keep unresolved/refuted conditions, blocked automata and action inhibition visible in refusal traces. Do not treat confidence as policy authority.

## Compatibility seams: preserve, do not reuse as new policy

- Legacy confidence path uses a +0..2 intensity boost, before final jitter, and defaults threshold62/max10/jitter1.5 (`engine.rs:754-772`; `mutate.rs:861-874`). This conflicts with the new frozen negative-valence penalty/defaults; keep it compatibility-only.
- Legacy `pick` uniformly selects when total≤0 (`engine.rs:181-199`), conflicting with frozen redundant zero-total abstention. Legacy grouping key is origin+normalized action and attached contributions receive unique grouping keys (`engine.rs:825-853`); this lacks exact captures, canonical AST and inhibition-set equality. Do not route new grouping through it.
- Legacy traversal hard/soft schedules lexical binding.remainder with four-hop/seen guards (`engine.rs:790-817`). This is the named behavior to preserve, not an available v3 remainder API.
- Legacy ATP updates after output from whole-input normalized substring Chargebook lookup, using wall milliseconds, unbounded elapsed, intensity-only release at `>.85`, and no valence release (`engine.rs:934-954`); it is not the new advance equation or clause/quote policy.
- Legacy persisted settings admit maxStrength up to1000, have JSON schema `wdbx.studio.v1` and ATP valence/intensity/lastUpdate (`mutate.rs:544-562,861-874`); existing IDs are checked using legacy identify (`:744-755`). No automatic settings/ATP/ID migration or saved-schema edit is justified by the pure tranche.

## Root rulings required before a concrete brief

1. **Profile boundary:** explicitly choose additive opt-in modules and new defaults, leaving legacy engine/save behavior intact. Frozen scoring supersedes old prose's open defaults for the new profile only.
2. **Bypass meaning:** `SimilarityBypass::Declared` says no ordinary similarity gate, but supplies no substitute Cbase/arbitration confidence. Decide whether bypass admits with retained scan Ceff, a separately declared score, or another explicit rule. Strength, inhibition, conditional, capability and budget gates must remain. Never silently assign100.
3. **Redundancy key:** decide common fanout lineage versus exact variant fanout_id; chunk identity includes input/clause/action. Define whether capture equality is typed values alone or also names/spans, and how an inhibition set is represented while current Alternative only has bool. Use selected action AST, not whole ActionAst weights, unless explicitly ruled otherwise. Attached groups remain exempt.
4. **Alternative draw semantics:** select proportional election and no-action zero total for entry alternatives as well as redundant groups, stable order/tie boundary and exact draw consumption (including singleton and protected-jitter cases). Frozen bundle specifies redundant zero total; architecture leaves source-alternative zero policy open.
5. **ATP cycle timing/aggregation:** choose whether fresh stimulus advances ATP before scoring or only supplies the next cycle; specify multiple charge matches, overlapping phrases, fanout deduplication and explicit urgent-only stimulus intensity. Current immutable Charge has rule_ref/valence/intensity/span (`language_v3/model.rs:209-227`), not an aggregate reducer. Define reward mapping and tonal charge-distance metric; neither is executable as written. Cooldown-crossing steps should follow the frozen branch at step entry unless explicitly revised.
6. **Attachments and scheduler scope:** select trigger policy, affinity mapping, depth/visit/progress and remainder representation. Freeze pure scheduler admission unit (candidate-origin scan vs concrete entry), treatment of a whole job that exceeds remaining queue capacity, deadline vs cancellation result, quantum completion order and permit release ownership. These must be explicit for S12-S15 rather than inferred from legacy.
7. **Plan integrity:** choose validated linkage of source PreparedAction, immutable target preparation, node policy and registry snapshot; define public-input consistency checking and aggregate work/draw/trace/output budgets. Public Candidate/Constraints/Evidence fields are data, not authenticated permits. These validations are local invariants, not real host authorization.
